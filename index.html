Frequently Asked Questions

How does Reviewly analyze app reviews?

Reviewly uses advanced AI algorithms to analyze app reviews from the App Store, providing sentiment analysis, trend identification, and actionable insights to help you understand user feedback patterns.

Can I analyze apps from different countries?

Yes! Reviewly supports global App Store search, allowing you to analyze app reviews from any country and understand regional market differences and user preferences.

What can I do with the Reviewly MCP server?

The MCP server lets compatible AI agents and developer tools query Reviewly insights, including review summaries, opportunities, sentiment patterns, and competitive research.

How do I contact support?

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. All data stays on your device and is never shared with third parties. You can delete all data by simply deleting the app and clearing your iCloud account.
